Common questions

Everything you want to know

Event Photography

What kinds of events do you shoot?

Prom nights, grad parties, corporate events, social gatherings, birthday parties — if it's a group of people having a good time, we're comfortable shooting it. We're based in Vancouver and know the best locations across Metro Vancouver and the Lower Mainland.

What's included in an event shoot?

Every event shoot includes group photos, individual portraits, fully edited and colour-corrected images, and a private online gallery for download. For larger events we bring a second shooter so nothing gets missed. Delivery is within 7–10 business days.

Do you shoot limo and vehicle photos?

Yes — limo coverage is available as part of our full event packages. We handle interior shots as well as outdoor stops along the route. If you have specific stops in mind, let us know when you reach out and we can plan accordingly.

Can you suggest good photo locations in Vancouver?

Absolutely. For outdoor event photos, Gastown and the Harbour area are our home turf. Other great spots depending on your route include Burrard Bridge, the Plaza of Nations, and North Vancouver waterfront. We'll suggest the best options based on your timing and itinerary.

How many people can you cover in one shoot?

Comfortably up to 15–20 people solo. For larger groups or when you want simultaneous group and individual coverage, we bring a second shooter. Just let us know your group size when you enquire and we'll recommend the right package.

How quickly do we get our photos?

Standard delivery is 7–10 business days. If you need photos sooner, ask about rush turnaround when booking — we can sometimes accommodate 48-hour delivery depending on the shoot scope.

Headshots & Portrait Sessions

Where do portrait sessions take place?

We shoot outdoors in natural light, or in a naturally lit indoor location that suits your look. We know Vancouver's best spots for clean, professional results — we'll recommend a location based on what you're going for.

Do you offer team headshot packages?

Yes. We offer group rates for teams of 3 or more. Reach out with your headcount and we'll put together a custom quote — usually easier and faster than everyone booking separately.

How many people can come to a family session?

The standard family package covers up to 5 people. For larger families or extended family sessions, just ask — we can accommodate larger groups and will adjust the pricing accordingly.

Booking & Logistics

How far in advance should I book?

For events, 2–4 weeks notice is ideal, especially for prom and grad season (May–June). For portrait sessions, 1–2 weeks is usually enough. We can sometimes accommodate shorter notice — just reach out and ask.

How do I confirm a booking?

A deposit via e-transfer confirms your date. For event packages the deposit is 30% of the total, with the remainder due on the day of the shoot. Deposits are non-refundable.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Vancouver and cover Metro Vancouver and the Lower Mainland — including Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, North Shore, Coquitlam, and surrounding areas.

What happens if it rains?

For outdoor portrait sessions we'll monitor the forecast together and reschedule at no charge if conditions aren't right. For events, we work with whatever the weather gives us — we'll adapt locations and timing as needed.

How do I pay?

E-transfer. A deposit is required to confirm your booking, with the remainder due on the day of the shoot. We'll send you the details when you book.

What's your cancellation policy?

Deposits are non-refundable. If you need to reschedule with more than 7 days notice, we'll do our best to find a new date at no charge. Last-minute cancellations may forfeit the full booking amount depending on the circumstances.

Still have questions? Just reach out.

We respond same day. No automated replies, no waiting in a queue.
