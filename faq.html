Restaurant & Brand Content

How does the monthly package actually work?
We schedule a shoot at your restaurant once a month. You tell us what's new — new dishes, seasonal menu, events — and we build a shot list around that. We show up, shoot, edit, and deliver a private gallery within 48 hours. You download what you need and use it however you like.
Do I need to write a creative brief or come prepared?
No. That's the point of working with us monthly — we handle the creative direction. Just let us know your new dishes and any upcoming events, and we take care of the rest. The less work it is for you, the better the arrangement works.
What does a typical shoot cover?
We cover hero food shots, atmospheric dining room and bar images, team and staff portraits, and any behind-the-scenes moments worth capturing. The mix depends on your package — the Standard and Full Coverage tiers give us more time to go deeper on each area.
Can I do a one-off shoot instead of a monthly package?
Yes. If you have a menu launch, grand opening, special event, or just need a content refresh, we can do a one-off shoot. Reach out and we'll build a custom quote based on what you need.
How long is the contract?
There's no long-term contract. All packages are month-to-month. You can pause or cancel with one month's notice. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.
What formats do I get the images in?
All images are delivered in a private online gallery optimised for Instagram (square and portrait crops), Instagram Stories, Google Business cover, and high-resolution print-ready files. You get everything in one gallery — no extra requests needed.

Headshots & Family Sessions

Where do headshot sessions take place?
We shoot outdoors in natural light, or in a naturally lit indoor location that suits your brand. We know Vancouver's best spots for clean, professional results — we'll recommend a location based on what look you're going for.
Do you offer team headshot packages?
Yes. We offer group rates for teams of 3 or more. Reach out with your headcount and we'll put together a custom quote — usually easier and faster than everyone booking separately.
How many people can come to a family session?
The standard family package covers up to 5 people. For larger families or extended family sessions, just ask — we can accommodate larger groups and will adjust the pricing accordingly.

Booking & Logistics

How far in advance should I book?
For monthly retainer clients, we schedule recurring slots that stay consistent. For one-off shoots and individual sessions, 1–2 weeks' notice is usually enough, though we can sometimes accommodate shorter notice depending on availability.
What areas do you cover?
We're based in Vancouver and cover Metro Vancouver and the Lower Mainland — including Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, North Shore, Coquitlam, and surrounding areas.
What happens if it rains?
For restaurant shoots, we're shooting indoors so weather isn't a factor. For outdoor portrait sessions (headshots, family), we'll monitor the forecast together and reschedule at no charge if conditions aren't right.
How do I pay?
Monthly retainer clients are invoiced at the start of each month. For one-off shoots and portrait sessions, a 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking, with the remainder due on the day of the shoot.
