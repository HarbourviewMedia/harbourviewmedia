Common questions

Quick answers

Do I need experience posing?

No — we direct every shot. Just show up and we'll handle the rest. Most people feel awkward at first and that's completely normal. Give it 10 minutes and it clicks.

Can I bring a friend or family member?

Yes. One support person is welcome at no charge. If you want them in photos too, book the Duo Session.

What do I wear?

Whatever feels like you. Cap and gown for the formal shots, then whatever you'd actually wear on a good day — that's where the best photos happen.

What if it rains?

We'll monitor the forecast together and reschedule at no charge if conditions aren't right. Vancouver weather is unpredictable — we're flexible.

How do I pay?

A deposit via e-transfer confirms your booking. The remaining balance is due on the day of your session.

How far in advance should I book?

Grad season (May–July) fills up fast. 2–3 weeks minimum is ideal. If your date is coming up sooner, reach out anyway — we'll do our best.
