How pricing works

What affects the quote

01

Duration

Half-day, full-day, or multi-day. The longer the coverage, the more scalable the team we deploy.

02

Team size

Larger events or bigger headshot rosters require more shooters. Volume discounts apply at scale.

03

Turnaround

Standard is 48 hours. Same-day or next-morning rush delivery is available at a premium.

04

Licensing scope

Standard corporate license covers internal use and owned channels. Broader commercial or ad licensing is quoted separately.
